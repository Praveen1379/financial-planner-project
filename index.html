Privacy

What's stored: the profile numbers, expenses, budgets and goals you enter, plus your email address. Where: your own document in this app's Firebase Firestore database, readable only while you're signed in as you. What isn't stored: bank logins, card numbers, account numbers — the app never asks for them. Export or delete your data any time using the buttons above. Deleting your data removes the stored document; deleting the account itself is done from your login provider.

This is a planning and education tool, not financial advice. Every projection is an estimate produced from the numbers and assumptions you enter — change an assumption and the result changes. Returns shown for schemes are historical or indicative and do not predict future performance. Verify anything important with a qualified adviser before acting on it.
